Privacy Policy

GeoVatz · Last updated: November 12, 2026

We don't sell your information

GeoVatz doesn't sell, rent or trade your personal or financial information. Ever.

What stays on your iPhone

Your accounts, transactions, goals, trips and settings are stored on your device, encrypted with keys kept in the iOS Keychain. GeoVatz has no servers that receive this data.

iCloud (optional)

Chats and optional Pro backups use Apple's CloudKit and encrypted fields. Shared chat participants can access the shared records. Party plans, bills, votes and notes remain local; sharing its chat does not synchronize its plan. Chat financial confirmations are also retained in your encrypted local vault and backups. Reports are optional emails: only when you send one does support receive the selected content and identifiers. Do not include unrelated financial information.

Artificial intelligence

AI features use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. Your questions and numbers are not sent to GeoVatz.

No ads, no tracking

GeoVatz shows no ads and includes no advertising or analytics software. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Internet lookups

When you scan a product barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product's name. Exchange rates are downloaded from public sources. No personal information is included in either of these.

Permissions

The camera (receipts and barcodes), microphone and speech recognition (voice expenses), notifications, calendar, reminders and Face ID are requested only when you use the feature that needs them. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

Your control

Profile lets you export or erase local data, and separately delete owned iCloud chats and leave other shared chats. Leaving a chat does not delete the owner’s copy. Previously downloaded copies and separate backups remain. Delete backups in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Deleting the app removes its local data, not its CloudKit records. Reports you send remain in support email; contact support to request their deletion.

Children

GeoVatz is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012 or US state laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Because your data lives on your device, you can do all of this directly in the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in an important way, the app will ask you to review it. Questions: use the contact information on GeoVatz's App Store page.
