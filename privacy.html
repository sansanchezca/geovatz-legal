Privacy Policy

GeoVatz · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who we are

GeoVatz is made by Santiago Sanchez, an independent developer. Contact: santiagosanchezcst@gmail.com.

We don't sell your information

GeoVatz doesn't sell, rent or trade your personal or financial information, and never shares your financial information with advertisers.

What stays on your iPhone

Unless you share something in a chat or Party, your accounts, transactions, goals, trips and settings are stored on your device, encrypted with keys kept in the iOS Keychain. GeoVatz has no servers that receive this data, and the developer can't see it.

iCloud (optional)

Chats and optional Pro backups use Apple's CloudKit in your own iCloud account, with encrypted fields. The developer can't read them. In a shared chat, the people you invite see the messages, money records and the name you choose, and Apple shows them the iCloud account that shared the chat. When you share a Party, its group chat sends the Party's name and everything you send in it (bill splits, money requests, contributions, amounts, notes and receipts you choose to send) to its members through CloudKit; the rest of the plan (checklists, budgets, votes and notes) stays on your iPhone. Chat financial confirmations are also retained in your encrypted local vault and backups. Chat notifications are delivered by Apple Push Notification service.

Purchases

GeoVatz Pro is sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; GeoVatz never receives your card or payment details, only whether your subscription is active.

Artificial intelligence

AI features use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. Your questions and numbers are not sent to GeoVatz. If you build a Shortcut with "Get Money Summary" (Pro), the summary goes only to the app or service you choose in that Shortcut (for example ChatGPT), and that service's privacy policy applies.

Camera and voice

Receipts and barcodes are read on your iPhone. Voice expenses are recognized on your iPhone. Photos and recordings are not uploaded or kept.

Internet lookups

When you scan a product barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find the product's name. Exchange rates are downloaded from ExchangeRate-API. No personal information is included in either of these.

Ads and tracking

GeoVatz doesn't track you across apps or websites and never asks for tracking permission. Free users see ad cards marked "Ad" in Home and Activity, and optional reward videos in the AI coach, provided by Google AdMob. They are non-personalized: GeoVatz sends Google no financial information, and Google may receive device and usage information (such as IP address, device type and ad interactions) to show the video, measure it and prevent fraud, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). In the EU and UK you're asked for consent first. Pro users never see ads. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Permissions

The camera (receipts and barcodes), microphone and speech recognition (voice expenses), notifications, calendar, reminders and Face ID are requested only when you use the feature that needs them. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.

How long data is kept

Local data stays until you erase it or delete the app. iCloud chats and backups stay in your iCloud until you delete them. Reports you email stay in the support inbox until you ask us to delete them.

Your control

Profile lets you export or erase local data, and separately delete owned iCloud chats and leave other shared chats. Leaving a chat does not delete the owner’s copy. Previously downloaded copies and separate backups remain. Delete backups in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. Deleting the app removes its local data, not its CloudKit records.

Reports

Reports are optional emails: only when you send one does support receive the selected content and identifiers. Do not include unrelated financial information. Reports are used only to review abuse and protect users.

Children

GeoVatz is not directed to children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK GDPR, California law or Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or move your data and to object to its use. Because your data lives on your device and in your iCloud, you can do most of this directly in the app. For anything else, email santiagosanchezcst@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, the app will ask you to review it.
