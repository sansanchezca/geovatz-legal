Privacy Policy

GeoVatz · Last updated: November 12, 2026

Who we are

GeoVatz is made by Santiago Sanchez, an independent developer. Contact: santiagosanchezcst@gmail.com.

On your iPhone

What you enter (transactions, spaces, pockets, cards, goals, trips, Parties and settings) is stored on your iPhone, encrypted. GeoVatz has no servers that receive it.

iCloud

Chats, shared Parties and optional Pro backups use Apple's CloudKit in your own iCloud account, with encrypted fields. People you invite to a chat or Party see what is shared there: messages, money records, receipts you send, Party names and the name you choose. Notifications are delivered by Apple.

Ads (free plan)

Free users see ads from Google AdMob: ad cards marked "Ad" and optional reward videos. Ads are not personalized and GeoVatz doesn't ask for tracking permission or send your financial information to Google. To show and measure ads, Google's SDK automatically collects device identifiers, approximate location from your IP address, ads you saw and tapped, app performance and crash data. Google uses identifiers for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). In the EU, UK and some US states you're asked for consent and can change it in Profile › Ad privacy choices. Pro has no ads.

Purchases

GeoVatz Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment; GeoVatz only learns whether your subscription is active.

Other services

AI features use Apple Intelligence on your iPhone. Barcode lookups send only the barcode number to Open Food Facts. Exchange rates are downloaded from ExchangeRate-API. If you build a Shortcut with "Get Money Summary" (Pro), the summary goes to the app you choose in that Shortcut, under its own privacy policy.

Camera, microphone and permissions

Receipts, barcodes and voice expenses are processed on your iPhone. Camera, microphone, speech recognition, notifications, calendar, reminders and Face ID are requested only when you use the feature that needs them and can be turned off in iOS Settings.

Reports

If you report a chat message, an email draft opens. Support receives the content only if you send it.

Keeping and deleting data

Local data stays until you erase it in Profile or delete the app. iCloud chats and backups stay in your iCloud until you delete them in the app or in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Leaving a chat doesn't delete the owner's copy. To delete a report you sent, email support.

Children

GeoVatz is not directed to children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (EU/UK GDPR, US state laws, Colombia's Ley 1581 de 2012) you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data. Most of it is on your device and in your iCloud, so you can do this in the app. For anything else, email santiagosanchezcst@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, the app will ask you to review it.
